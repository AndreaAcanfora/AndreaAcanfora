### Hi, I'm Andrea 👋

**Product engineer · Web3 & AI · TypeScript / React**  
Florence, Italy · fully remote, CET with flexible overlap on US hours

I build the frontend of [Routescan](https://routescan.io) and [Snowtrace](https://snowtrace.io), a family of blockchain explorers serving **millions of monthly users** across **dozens of chains**, with **hundreds of transactions per second** of live data. Lately I also work on the AI side: the streaming chat assistant on Snowtrace that answers questions about live on-chain data through tool calls.

11 years shipping production web software, the last four in Web3. Before Routescan I was VP of Engineering at Avascan.

### What I'm good at

- **Data-heavy frontends at scale.** Server-rendered Next.js (App Router, RSC), caching and revalidation, Core Web Vitals, pagination for datasets too large to fetch eagerly.
- **Wallet and on-chain UX.** Sign-in by wallet signature, message signing, read/write interaction with verified contracts, on Viem, Wagmi and RainbowKit.
- **LLM product interfaces.** Streaming chat with tool calls, page-aware context, saved conversations and answer feedback.
- **Owning the whole loop.** Jest and Playwright in CI, preview deploys, Sentry and Amplitude in production. At Avascan I led the frontend team and reviewed every pull request.
